# 🛒 Kadam Mart Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI 📊

An interactive **Power BI Business Intelligence Dashboard** designed to analyze Kadam Mart retail sales data and transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights. This dashboard enables decision-makers to monitor key performance indicators, analyze customer behavior, evaluate product performance, and track regional sales through interactive visualizations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Kadam Mart's retail sales data using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance, profitability, customer segmentation, product analysis, and geographical trends to support data-driven business decisions.

The project demonstrates practical skills in:

- Data Cleaning & Transformation (Power Query)
- Data Modeling
- DAX Calculations
- Business Intelligence Reporting
- Dashboard Design
- Data Visualization
- KPI Development
- Interactive Report Design

---

## 📈 Key Business Questions Solved

- What is the overall Revenue, Profit, Orders, and Return Rate?
- How has revenue changed over time?
- Which brands generate the highest number of orders?
- Which products contribute the most revenue?
- Which customers generate the highest revenue?
- How do customer membership and income levels affect sales?
- Which countries and regions perform the best?
- What is the monthly business performance?
- How do price adjustments impact product profitability?
- Which products have the highest return percentage?

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Revenue reached **$1.8M** with a profit of **$1.1M**.
- More than **269K orders** were processed with an overall **1% return rate**.
- Revenue shows a steady upward trend throughout the analysis period.
- Hermanos is the highest-performing brand by order volume.
- Customer segmentation reveals purchasing patterns across membership and income levels.
- Geographic analysis highlights sales distribution across the USA, Canada, and Mexico.
- The What-If Price Adjustment analysis helps evaluate the impact of pricing changes on profitability.
- Top-performing products consistently contribute a significant portion of total revenue.
